CONTROL 5: REDUNDANCY
=====================

Biological comparison:
Ethylene ERF11 vs Air ERF11

Purpose:
Assess whether differential-expression effect estimates are robust
to analysis with a second established RNA-seq method.

Primary method:
DESeq2

Redundant method:
edgeR using filterByExpr, TMM normalization, dispersion estimation,
and quasi-likelihood testing.

Why filterByExpr was used:
edgeR recommends filtering genes with insufficient expression before
dispersion estimation and multiple-testing correction. This reduces
the contribution of genes with little statistical information.

Important limitation:
DESeq2 and edgeR are not fully independent because they share the
same raw counts, biological samples, treatment labels and general
negative-binomial framework. Upstream errors such as incorrect sample
labels could therefore affect both approaches in the same way.
