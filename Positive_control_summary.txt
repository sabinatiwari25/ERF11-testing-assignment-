CONTROL 4: POSITIVE CONTROL
===========================

Purpose:
Artificially introduce known expression differences into genes
that originally showed little evidence of an Air/Ethylene effect.

Signal planted:
Counts for the same 20 genes were multiplied in all three
ethylene samples by factors corresponding to planted log2FC
values of 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, and 3.

Preserved:
- Original raw count matrix structure
- Six samples
- Three Air and three Ethylene samples
- Original biological variability
- Gene identities
- DESeq2 analysis method
- Filtering rule
- Original DEG criteria

Selected planted genes: 20
